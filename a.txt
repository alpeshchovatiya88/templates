Loss Estimation and Risk-Based Pricing (RBP)

For the validation dataset, estimated losses are generated for Mortgage Insurance (MI) and LRU+ loans using SMI 3.0. These losses support risk ranking and risk-based pricing (RBP). Unlike SMI 2.0, which produces monthly cashflows, SMI 3.0 generates total estimated losses. To maintain compatibility with the existing RBP framework, total losses are distributed across monthly periods using the corresponding MI or LRU+ loss curves.

The monthly loss calculation is defined as:

Loss Estimate_RBP_Month = Estimated Loss × Loss Curve Vector (MI or LRU+)

For LRU+, the business team developed a standardized loss curve based on SMI 2.0 loss patterns for funded loans from the 2025 book year. Double exponential smoothing was applied to reduce volatility while preserving the 120-month loss distribution, as illustrated in Figure XX.

Figure XX: LRU+ Loss Pattern – Original vs. Smoothed Curve

The business team’s validation confirmed that the smoothed curve has minimal pricing impact, with approximately 96% of loans experiencing premium rate changes within ±1 basis point. Although SMI 3.0 generates higher estimated losses than SMI 2.0, primarily due to updated property-level distress factors, subsequent pricing assessments showed no material impact on average premiums or ROE. Accordingly, the business team concluded that SMI 3.0 losses are appropriate for LRU+ RBP without further calibration.
