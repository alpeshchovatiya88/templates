BL presented the completed multi-phase business validation of SMI 3.0, including a review of 2,400 files with an overall underwriter agreement rate of 82%.
The model was simplified from 18 to 12 features to improve consistency, transparency and explainability while maintaining predictive performance.
BL discussed refinements to debt treatment, risk factors and monotone constraints to improve model stability and risk differentiation.
RD presented hyperparameter optimization results, which reduced model instability from approximately 13% to 4%, with broadly consistent results across testing methodologies.
BL highlighted that changes to economic scenario assumptions require reassessment, validation and recalibration. The team completed over 50 model iterations and continued final testing and documentation.
BL presented LGD refinements, including enhanced property-level distress factors, expense treatment and mortgage risk considerations.
BL compared SMI 2.0 and SMI 3.0, demonstrating broadly similar overall results with improved risk separation and limited movement in risk rankings.
BL discussed the revised credit-score approach using TransUnion TrueVision as the primary score and FICO 10 as backup, along with regional impacts, particularly in Atlantic Canada.
JB presented the integration of SMI 3.0 into the existing LRU Plus pricing framework, with minimal overall premium impact; approximately 80% of loans showed no premium change.
RD confirmed completion of major technical validation checks, with remaining findings primarily related to documentation, rationale and acceptance criteria.
CS, SL, AC, EO and WM agreed to approve business validation, subject to completion of final documentation at the next MSC meeting.
