Meeting Highlights

* BL presented SMI 3.0 business validation results, including 2,400 files reviewed (82% underwriter agreement) and feature reduction from 18 to 12, improving consistency and explainability while maintaining predictive performance.
* BL outlined refinements to debt treatment, risk factors, LGD calculations and model constraints to improve stability and risk differentiation.
* RD presented hyperparameter optimization results demonstrating improved model stability and confirmed SMI 3.0’s use for file-level risk ranking under the 60th-percentile economic scenario only, excluding loss forecasting.
* BL compared SMI 2.0 and 3.0, highlighting improved risk separation, updated credit scoring (TrueVision and FICO 10) and regional impacts, particularly in Atlantic Canada.
* JB presented SMI 3.0 integration into the LRU Plus pricing framework, with minimal premium impact and approximately 80% of loans showing no change.
* RD provided the technical validation update, confirming completion of major checks, with outstanding documentation and testing requirements.
* CS, SL, AC, EO and WM conditionally approved business validation, subject to completion of final validation activities and documentation.

Discussion Summary

* Model Performance and Stability: The committee discussed model simplification, stability improvements and the trade-off between predictive performance and explainability.
* Model Usage: RD clarified that SMI 3.0 is intended for file-level risk ranking under the approved economic scenario; any scenario changes require reassessment and recalibration.
* Risk Differentiation: The committee reviewed LGD refinements, updated credit scoring and improved risk rankings compared with SMI 2.0.
* Regional Impact: The committee requested an urban-rural breakdown of Atlantic results to support automated decision-threshold setting.
* Pricing Impact: The committee reviewed the minimal impact of SMI 3.0 on LRU Plus premiums.
* Validation and Approval: The committee reviewed outstanding validation requirements and granted conditional business-validation approval, with final documentation to be presented at the next MSC meeting.
