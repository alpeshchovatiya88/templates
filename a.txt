The model data are constructed through a centralized CI/CD preprocessing pipeline that integrates multiple upstream sources, including Snowflake DM_POL policy and borrower data, credit information, economic data, claims/delinquency data, LRU Plus data, and geographic/FSA information. Policy and borrower information are processed separately through the PolicyProcessor and BorrowerProcessor, with borrower credit information consolidated across available credit sources before being aggregated and integrated with policy information at the APPLICATION_NUMBER level.

Following application-level integration, applicable population-selection criteria and business filters are applied to construct the final model dataset, which is subsequently segmented into the training, testing, and recent validation populations as illustrated in Figure 2.1.1. The preprocessing implementation includes controls such as required-field validation, missing-identifier handling, duplicate identification/removal, and intermediate logging, which support traceability of the data through the preprocessing process. 

Overall, validation observed that the preprocessing implementation provides an identifiable and traceable source-to-model data lineage, with key upstream sources, processing components, integration keys, and final model outputs defined within the pipeline.
