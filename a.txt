Data quality and record-level controls. The raw policy extract contained 6,297,845 records and 64 fields, while the raw borrower extract contained approximately 10.29 million records and 57 fields. The policy preprocessing identified 1,007 duplicate application records, and the policy dataset contained 6,281,628 records following cleanup. The resulting MI population contained 2,249,154 records with no duplicate applications identified. ETD claim/delinquency data also contained repeated episodes (41,710 duplicate episodes), which were aggregated to the application level, resulting in 128,052 records. These results demonstrate that duplicate identification and application-level aggregation controls are incorporated into preprocessing prior to model population construction.

Policy and borrower integration. Following the policy transformations and combination of MI and LRUPLUS populations, the processed policy population contained 2,515,477 records. Borrower and external credit information were separately cleaned, combined and aggregated to application level, resulting in approximately 6.28 million application-level borrower records. Policy and borrower information was subsequently merged, producing the combined model dataset. The validation review observed that the preprocessing pipeline therefore performs transformations at the appropriate policy/borrower level before constructing the application-level modelling population.

Missing-value treatment. Missingness was explicitly evaluated before and after preprocessing. Prior to final model-data construction, missing observations were identified in several fields, including CRA_BANKRUPTCY_FORECLOSURE_IND, CRD_SCORE_bin, POD_MARKET_CODE, PRK_PROPERTY_AGE, PRD_PORT, and NUM_OF_BOR. Records missing the specified required fields were removed during the policy/borrower consolidation stage. For the training population, remaining missing values were observed principally in PRD_CONTRACT_RATE (29,105), POD_INSURANCE_IN_FORCE (17,620) and PRK_PROPERTY_VALUE (52). Following application of the pipeline’s missing-value treatment, the reviewed fields showed zero missing values. For the testing population, missing values included 69 observations each for TDSR, GDSR and DSR_DIFF, nine for insurance-in-force, and three for property value; the preprocessing procedure removed records with missing DSR_DIFF and applied the prescribed missing-value treatment, after which the reviewed fields showed zero missing values.

Outlier and range treatment. The validation team also reviewed the feature clipping procedure applied to continuous variables. In the training population, the largest observed range breaches were associated with GDSR (7,317; 0.9091%) and TDSR (5,331; 0.6623%), followed by total income (1,724; 0.2142%). Other reviewed variables had substantially lower breach rates, including loan term, property value, property age, qualifying interest rate, insurance-in-force and amortization, while ACTLTV showed no range breaches. In the testing population, the highest reported breach rate was for total income (0.2516%); the remaining reviewed variables exhibited relatively small or no breaches. The pipeline applies predefined lower and upper feature limits to these observations, providing consistent treatment of extreme values between development and testing datasets.

Business-rule transformations and population construction. In addition to generic cleansing, the preprocessing code applies model-specific business rules and transformations, including policy-status/product eligibility rules, MI/LRUPLUS population construction, claim/delinquency aggregation, borrower and credit-score consolidation, feature derivation, economic-data integration, and final population selection. Quantitative pipeline outputs provide additional evidence of these transformations: 169,673 records were removed by the product filter and 201,885 records by the refinance OSB filter. The resulting model populations contained 458,891 training observations, 317,170 testing observations, and 74,729 recent observations; the recent population covers EFFECT_SID 202510–202605.

Validation conclusion

Based on the independent code review and quantitative preprocessing checks, the data cleansing and transformation framework is generally systematic and traceable. Duplicate records, missing values and extreme observations are explicitly identified and treated, while model-specific eligibility and transformation rules are applied programmatically. The post-transformation checks provided show no remaining missing values for the reviewed model fields after the applicable cleansing procedures.
